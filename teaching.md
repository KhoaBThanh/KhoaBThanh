I teach in the Faculty of Commerce and Tourism at the Industrial University of
Ho Chi Minh City, where I have worked since 2009. The subjects are the
commerce and electronic commerce courses, together with the planning and
teamwork skills that students carry into industry.

I also teach and supervise at bachelor, master, and doctoral level as a
visiting lecturer and researcher.

## Courses

- Fundamental of Electronic Commerce
- Electronic Commerce Operation Management
- Teamwork Skills
- Planning Skills

## Supervision

Research supervision covers electronic commerce, consumer behaviour, and
marketing, at bachelor, master, and doctoral level. Students who look for a
topic will find current work on the
[publications page](/publications/).
